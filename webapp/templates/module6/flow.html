Optical flow

Farnebäck flow is drawn as a color wheel (hue is direction, brightness is speed) and as arrows. Sparse corners are tracked with pyramidal Lucas–Kanade. The clips below are saved with the assignment and play on the hosted site. Uploading a new video works only when this app is running on your computer: the hosted server keeps nothing between requests.

What the field is saying

Pick a saved clip, or run one locally. A still camera lights up only the moving objects. A moving camera lights up the whole frame, and nearer things move faster.
