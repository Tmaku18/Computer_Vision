What was measured

One phone slides a known distance \(B\). Depth is \(Z = f B / d\), and an edge length is the distance between the two back-projected endpoints. A rectangle reports width and height, a circle reports its diameter, and any other polygon reports every edge. The table below is the built-in plane, whose true sizes are known exactly, so the implementation can be checked before real photos replace it.

Computing…

A real room uses two clips. The long wall's horizontal span is the length, the short wall's horizontal span is the width, and the floor-to-ceiling plane distance is the height, measured once in each clip. Videos are processed locally with HW5/room_dimensions.py because a 30-second upload does not fit the hosted request limit. Video mode needs its own calibration (HW5/calibrate_video.py): the still-photo \(K\) does not describe a stabilized video frame.
