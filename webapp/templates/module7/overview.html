Module 7 · Stereo sizes and a room

Assignment 7 asks for the size of an object from two views, the depth first, then the size. The same phone slides a measured distance between the views. A separate part is a derivation you write by hand, and a third part measures a room from video.

1

Calibrated stereo

Click a rectangle, a circle, or another polygon. Depth comes from how far each point shifts, then the edges are real lengths.

 2

Room from video

Two still frames from a pause–slide–pause clip. The rail length sets the scale. The wall you face gives one side of the room, and the floor-to-ceiling gap gives the height.

Theory
