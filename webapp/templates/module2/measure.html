Step 2 · Real-world 2D dimensions

Clicked pixels are first undistorted with the calibrated lens model. With a reference on the object's plane, the perspective projection \(\lambda\,[u, v, 1]^\top = K\,[\,r_1\; r_2\; t\,]\,[X, Y, 1]^\top = H\,[X, Y, 1]^\top\) is inverted to get plane coordinates \((X, Y)\). With only the camera distance \(Z\), the pinhole equations \(X = Z(u - c_x)/f_x,\; Y = Z(v - c_y)/f_y\) are used instead.
