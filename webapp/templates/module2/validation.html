Step 3 · Validation: 20 measurements from more than 2 m

For each measurement click the two ends of a segment whose true length you measured with a tape, type that length, and add it. Rows are saved in this browser, can span several photos, and are exported as CSV for the report and for HW1/validate_measurements.py.

Experiment protocol
  1. Tape the printed checkerboard flat on a wall (the reference plane). Measure one square with a ruler.
  2. Tape or lean the objects flat against the same wall: boxes, books, sheets, tape strips of different lengths. Anything that sticks out from the wall adds error.
  3. Put the phone on a tripod or a fixed surface, pointing at the wall, and tape-measure the camera-to-wall distance: more than 2 m (e.g. 2.5 m). Record it below under “Camera distance”.
  4. Use the same zoom (1×), landscape orientation and camera as in Step 1. Keep objects inside the yellow circle (valid lens region).
  5. Measure 20 different segments (widths, heights, diagonals) with a tape measure and write them down before or after taking 1–3 photos.
  6. Here: upload a photo, detect the checkerboard, then add each segment with its tape length.
