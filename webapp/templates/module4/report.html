What was measured

A person is outlined twice from the same box. The classical side is OpenCV with no trained weights: GrabCut on color photos (a graph cut whose color models are fit to that photo alone), marker-based watershed as a second method, and background differencing when the camera does not move. Thermal images use CLAHE, Otsu, and a filter that keeps blobs with a person-like area and aspect ratio. The other side is SAM2, prompted with that same box and saved as a PNG. The hosted site never loads SAM2.

Scores are intersection-over-union, Dice, precision, recall, boundary F at a 2-pixel tolerance, Hausdorff distance, and the mean distance between the two boundaries. Hausdorff is the single worst pair of boundary pixels, so a filled gap under an arm can be tens of pixels even when IoU is above 0.98. The mean boundary distance is the typical miss. The overlay paints both methods green, OpenCV only blue, and SAM2 only red.

Where the classical outline loses

  • Hair and thin limbs. Morphological opening, which removes speckle, also erases strands that are thinner than the kernel. SAM2 keeps them because it was trained on people, not because the box told it the hair color.
  • Shadows and low contrast. GrabCut's color model treats a dark shadow as a second foreground color, or gives up when the shirt matches the wall. A thermal image has the same failure when the room is warm and the person is not much brighter.
  • Clutter inside the box. Everything outside the box is background, but anything inside can be claimed. A busy room inside a loose box is where watershed and GrabCut disagree with SAM2.
  • Touching people. Otsu sees one bright blob. The optional distance-transform split helps only when the join is thinner than the bodies.

Background differencing avoids the color ambiguity when the camera is fixed and the empty frame is available. It fails if the camera moves or the light changes between the two shots.

The masks shipped in HW3/samples/sam2/ are not SAM2. They are the synthetic figure eroded by a few pixels, so the overlay and the metrics have something to show before SAM2 is installed.
