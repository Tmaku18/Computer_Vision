Module 3 · Blurring in space and in frequency

A blur slides a small kernel across the image and replaces every pixel by a weighted average of its neighbors. The convolution theorem says the same picture comes out if you instead transform the image and the kernel with the Fourier transform, multiply them, and transform back. This module does both and checks that they agree.

Demo

Blur an image

Pick a kernel, see the spatial blur beside the Fourier blur, and read off the difference. It should be about 10−12, which is rounding error.
