Blur: spatial filter vs. Fourier multiply

The spatial result is a direct weighted sum of neighbors. The Fourier result multiplies transforms. They match when the borders match: zero padding for “linear”, wrap-around for “circular”.

Spectra

Log magnitude, low frequencies in the center. Blur keeps the bright center and wipes the edges of the spectrum.

Time vs. kernel size

Same Gaussian blur. The spatial sum slows down as the kernel grows; the FFT size barely changes.
